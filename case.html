Justice for Kylee

The Case

This page follows the criminal court proceedings connected to the death of Kylee M. Monteiro and will be updated as reliable, publicly available information becomes available.

Current Status

Commonwealth v. Gregory Groom

Gregory Groom of Rehoboth, Massachusetts has been charged in connection with the death of 18-year-old Kylee Monteiro.

Current publicly reported status:

A Bristol County grand jury indicted Gregory Groom in November 2025 on charges of murder and witness intimidation.

He was arraigned on the indictment in Fall River Superior Court on November 25, 2025. He pleaded not guilty and was ordered held without bail.

This remains a criminal prosecution. A charge or indictment is an accusation, not a conviction. Gregory Groom is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court.

Court Timeline

Key Publicly Reported Developments

August 19, 2025

Arrest & Recovery

Authorities announced charges against Gregory Groom relating to an alleged August 6 domestic violence incident involving Kylee. Later that day, investigators recovered human remains from the Rehoboth property where Groom lived.

August 20, 2025

District Court Arraignment

Groom appeared in Taunton District Court. Prosecutors presented their allegations concerning Kylee's death and the investigation. A not-guilty plea was entered, and Groom was ordered held without bail.

November 20, 2025

Grand Jury Indictment

The Bristol County District Attorney's Office announced that a grand jury had indicted Groom on charges of murder and witness intimidation, moving the prosecution to Superior Court.

November 25, 2025

Superior Court Arraignment

Groom was arraigned in Fall River Superior Court on the indictment. He pleaded not guilty and remained held without bail.

The Allegations

What Prosecutors Have Said in Court

The statements below describe prosecution allegations reported from court proceedings. They should not be confused with findings of guilt.

Kylee's Disappearance

According to prosecutors, Groom reported Kylee missing after she had been at his Rehoboth residence. Investigators subsequently focused their investigation on the property and the circumstances surrounding her disappearance.

The Investigation

Prosecutors told the court that Groom later made statements to investigators concerning Kylee's death and provided information about where investigators could locate her remains.

Recovery of Kylee

Investigators recovered Kylee's remains on the Rehoboth property on August 19, 2025, following information prosecutors say Groom provided during the investigation.

The Indictment

In November 2025, a Bristol County grand jury returned an indictment charging Groom with murder and witness intimidation. The indictment brought the case into Superior Court.

Important: The information on this page summarizes publicly reported court proceedings and statements attributed to prosecutors and law enforcement. Allegations are not findings of guilt. The defendant has pleaded not guilty and is entitled to the presumption of innocence throughout the criminal proceedings.
