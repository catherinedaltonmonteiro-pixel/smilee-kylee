Meet Kylee Our Mission
The Heart Behind Our Name

Kylee M. Monteiro

November 4, 2006 — August 19, 2025

Kylee was a sister, daughter, granddaughter, friend, and so much more.

She was funny. She could be silly. She could make a face at the camera one moment and look absolutely beautiful the next. She grew from a little girl holding her favorite things into a young woman with a life and future of her own.

Smilee Kylee exists because her life mattered. This organization will carry her name forward by helping women who need somewhere safe to land and someone willing to believe that their future is still worth fighting for.

Her story does not end with our loss. Her memory becomes hope.
A Life Remembered

Remembering Kylee as Kylee

Not only through the day we lost her, but through childhood, family, laughter, accomplishments, ordinary moments and the person she became.

The Future

A Safe Place to Begin Again

Smilee Kylee is being created to support women 18 and older who need housing, safety, structure, support, and a real opportunity to rebuild their lives.

01

Safe Housing

A stable and secure place where women can rest, breathe, and begin working toward their future.

02

Therapy & Healing

Structured participation in counseling and supportive services focused on emotional health and long-term stability.

03

Pregnancy Support

Support for pregnant women 18 and older who need safety, stability, education, resources, and preparation for motherhood.

04

Financial Education

Learning how to budget, save money, understand credit, manage expenses, and build financial independence.

05

Employment Support

Help with resumes, applications, job searching, interviews, workplace preparation, and creating dependable income.

06

Independent Living

A structured path toward permanent housing, confidence, stability, and independence beyond the shelter.

Looking for Safety?

You Deserve Somewhere Safe.

Smilee Kylee is currently being developed and is not yet an operating emergency shelter. As the organization grows, this page will include eligibility information, applications, available services, and safe ways to contact our team.

If you are in immediate danger: Call 911 or contact an established emergency or domestic violence service in your area. Please do not wait for Smilee Kylee to open if you need safety now.
