The Heart Behind Our Name

In Loving Memory of Kylee Monteiro

Kylee was a sister, a daughter, a friend, and someone whose life mattered deeply. Smilee Kylee is being created so that her memory can continue through something living — a place where women can find safety, support, dignity, and the opportunity to begin again.

“Kylee's story does not end with our loss. Her memory becomes hope.”

Our Mission

A Safe Place to Begin Again

Smilee Kylee is being built with a vision of providing housing and structured support for women 18 and older who are experiencing homelessness, unsafe living situations, abuse, pregnancy without adequate support, or other circumstances that leave them without a safe home.

01

Safety & Housing

A stable environment where women can rest, feel protected, and focus on rebuilding their lives.

02

Healing & Support

Structured participation in counseling and supportive services designed to encourage healing and long-term emotional wellness.

03

Pregnancy Support

Support for pregnant women 18 and older as they prepare for motherhood and work toward a stable future.

04

Financial Education

Practical education focused on budgeting, saving, credit, financial responsibility, and independence.

05

Employment

Assistance with resumes, job searching, interviews, workplace preparation, and building reliable income.

06

Independent Living

A structured path toward permanent housing, stability, confidence, and a life beyond the shelter.

The Journey Forward

From Surviving to Living

Our goal is not simply to provide a place to sleep. Our goal is to help each woman reach the day when she no longer needs us.

You Are Not Alone

Do You Need a Safe Place?

Smilee Kylee is still being developed and is not yet an operating emergency shelter. As we grow, this page will provide information about eligibility, applications, available services, and how to reach our team safely.

If you are in immediate danger: Call 911 or contact a trusted local emergency or domestic violence service. Do not wait for Smilee Kylee to open if you need safety now.
