Kylee M. Monteiro · November 4, 2006 — August 19, 2025

Kylee's Timeline

This timeline remembers the life Kylee lived, the final days that changed our family forever, the 12 days we searched for her, and the fight for justice and change that continues in her name.

Kylee's Life Final Days & The 12 Days Justice & What Came After
Part One

Kylee's Life

Kylee's story did not begin with a missing-person report or a court case. She was a daughter, sister, niece, granddaughter, friend, student, welder, graduate, and expectant mother. She spent much of her life overcoming things no child should have had to experience.

November 4, 2006

Kylee Is Born

Kylee M. Monteiro enters the world. She would grow into an outgoing, social and funny young woman whose family remembers her contagious laugh, her love of people, and her ability to make friends wherever she went.

June 2008

Kylee Loses Her Father

Kylee loses her father while she is still a baby. It is the first of profound losses she would experience while growing up.

Childhood

Growing Up Through Hardship

Kylee experiences poverty during childhood. Even through difficult circumstances, she is loved by her family and grows into a girl who loves being around people, making friends and making others laugh.

December 2019 · Age 13

Kylee Loses Her Mother

Kylee's mother dies three days before Christmas. After this devastating loss, family members open their home to Kylee and her sister Hailey.

Teen Years

Mental Health, Change & Resilience

Kylee struggles with her mental health while continuing to grow into the funny, social and deeply loved young woman her family knew. Her story includes hardship, but hardship was never the whole of who she was.

Summer Before Senior Year

Kylee Discovers Welding

Kylee begins welding and loves it. The nickname Smilee Kylee becomes connected to this chapter of her life—a name that now carries her memory forward.

2025

Prom Queen

During her senior year, Kylee is chosen as Prom Queen—one of the joyful milestones her family wants remembered alongside everything that came later.

2025 · Age 18

Kylee Graduates High School

Kylee graduates from Attleboro High School. She is 18 years old, newly graduated and standing at the beginning of adult life.

Summer 2025

Kylee Learns She Is Pregnant

Kylee learns she is expecting her first child. Her family saw the pregnancy give her renewed purpose and something to look forward to. If she had a daughter, Kylee had already chosen the name Eleanor—Ella for short.

She never got the chance to hold her baby, but she was already dreaming about motherhood.

Summer 2025

Kylee Needs Safety and Stability

Pregnant and experiencing housing instability and mental-health challenges, Kylee needs somewhere safe to go. She spends time in an inpatient psychiatric program after experiencing suicidal thoughts and later stays at Bethesda House in Pembroke.

Family account
Part Two

Final Days & The 12 Days

Some events in this section come from Kylee's family and their direct experience; others reflect allegations made by prosecutors. Where questions remain, this page leaves them as questions rather than presenting assumptions as fact.

Early August 2025

Kylee Leaves Bethesda House

According to her family's understanding, Kylee is discharged from Bethesda House after missing a required meeting following a hospital visit. She is then transported back to Gregory Groom's home in Rehoboth.

Her family continues to seek answers about who made that decision, what information was available at the time, and whether known safety concerns were considered.

Family account · Questions remain
August 6, 2025

Kylee Reaches Out to Her Sister

Kylee communicates with her sister and describes an alleged physical assault by Gregory Groom. The family later identifies an approximately 30-minute period between Kylee's frightening first communication and a later message saying she was safe as one of the periods they want fully explained.

Family account · Allegation
August 7, 2025

Kylee Disappears

Kylee disappears. She is 18 years old, newly graduated and approximately 11 weeks pregnant. Prosecutors later allege that Groom killed Kylee. Groom has pleaded not guilty.

Criminal allegations remain pending
August 8, 2025

Kylee Is Reported Missing

Gregory Groom reports Kylee missing. Her family enters a period of fear, searching and uncertainty that will define the next 12 days.

August 8–19, 2025

The Search for Kylee

Kylee's family searches, hopes and prays for her return. Law enforcement and search teams use multiple resources in efforts to locate her. Groom also participates in efforts surrounding the search.

During these days, the family experiences events and communications that remain important to their understanding of what happened, including an apparent kidnapping/hostage call and statements made while everyone was searching.

Family experience · Some questions remain unanswered
During the Search

An Earlier Search of the Property

The Rehoboth property is searched before Kylee is ultimately recovered, but she is not found during that earlier search. For Kylee's family, why she was not located sooner remains an important question.

August 19, 2025

Kylee Is Found

Investigators recover Kylee's remains from the Rehoboth property. Prosecutors later allege that Groom admitted killing Kylee and provided investigators with a hand-drawn map showing where she had been buried.

Kylee and her unborn baby are gone. Her family is left with grief, questions and a determination to fight for justice.

Prosecutor allegation · Groom has pleaded not guilty

For 12 days, we searched for our sister.
For 12 days, we hoped we would get her back.
For 12 days, we refused to give up.

We will never forget those 12 days.

Part Three

Justice & What Came After

Kylee's death became a criminal case, but her family believes the fight must also be about accountability, awareness and building better support for vulnerable women.

August 20, 2025

District Court Arraignment

Gregory Groom is arraigned in connection with the case. He pleads not guilty and is held without bail.

November 2025

Grand Jury Indictment

A Bristol County grand jury indicts Groom on charges including murder and witness intimidation in connection with Kylee's death.

An indictment is an accusation, not a conviction
November 25, 2025

Superior Court Arraignment

Groom is arraigned in Fall River Superior Court. He pleads not guilty and remains held without bail.

August 19, 2026 · One Year Without Kylee

The Website Was Supposed to Launch

Kylee's family had planned to release the Smilee Kylee website on the first anniversary of the day she was found.

The goal was to make sure Kylee would be remembered for more than how she died—to preserve who she was, continue the fight for justice, and begin creating the kind of support for women that her family wishes had existed for Kylee.

One year after we lost Kylee, we wanted her story to begin creating change.

Family milestone
September 2026

Defense Requests a Plea Resolution

According to information provided to Kylee's family, Groom's attorney requested a plea resolution that would reduce the case from murder to manslaughter, with a proposed 20-year sentence and the possibility of parole.

Kylee's family opposes resolving the case on those terms and wants the evidence surrounding Kylee's death fully heard and accountability that reflects what happened to Kylee and her unborn baby.

Information provided to Kylee's family · Not described here as an accepted plea agreement
Present

The Fight Continues

The criminal case remains part of Kylee's story, but it is not the end of her story. Her family continues to seek answers, advocate for justice, preserve Kylee's memory and build Smilee Kylee in her name.

About This Timeline

This page combines family knowledge and experience with information connected to the criminal case. Allegations against Gregory Groom are identified as allegations. He has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. As additional records, court information and firsthand documentation become available, this timeline can be expanded or corrected.

Her Story Does Not End Here.

Kylee needed safety, stability, compassion and someone willing to keep helping when life became difficult.

We cannot change what happened to Kylee. We can work to change what happens to the women who come after her.
