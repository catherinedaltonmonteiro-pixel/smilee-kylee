The Bigger Goal

What We're Working Toward

Our long-term goal is to build a shelter and support program for women that combines safe housing with practical, compassionate help.

Building Smilee Kylee Supporting the development and growth of the organization as we work toward making the larger vision possible.
Resources & Support Building connections, educational resources, and future programs designed to help women move toward stability.
The Future Shelter Working toward our ultimate goal of a safe home where women can heal, learn, rebuild, and prepare for independent living.
Important Information

Smilee Kylee Is Still Developing

Smilee Kylee is currently being built and is not yet operating a residential shelter or emergency housing program.

Smilee Kylee does not currently represent donations as tax-deductible. Information about charitable status will be updated as the organization develops.

We believe supporters deserve clear information about where Smilee Kylee is today and where we hope to take it tomorrow. This page will continue to be updated as the organization grows.
