If You Are Experiencing Abuse

Your Safety Comes First

Leaving an abusive situation can be complicated and sometimes dangerous. You do not have to make every decision today. A domestic violence advocate can help you think through your options and create a safety plan.

  • Use a safer phone or computer if you believe your device is being monitored.
  • Consider telling someone you trust what is happening.
  • Keep important documents, medication, keys, and emergency contacts accessible when possible.
  • Do not confront someone about leaving if doing so could increase your danger.
  • Contact a trained advocate for help making a plan specific to your situation.

What Smilee Kylee Believes

Help Should Not Disappear When Life Gets Messy

Smilee Kylee was created around a simple belief: people often need the most support during the moments when everything feels hardest.

Ask for Help You do not have to wait until everything has completely fallen apart.
Try Again A missed appointment, setback, or difficult day does not erase your worth.
Keep Going Progress may not happen in a straight line.
Important

Smilee Kylee Is Not Yet an Operating Shelter

Smilee Kylee is currently a developing memorial, advocacy, and future support project.

We are not yet operating an emergency shelter, residential program, crisis hotline, counseling service, or emergency response service.

Our long-term vision is to build supportive housing and practical programs for women, but anyone who needs immediate assistance should contact an established service listed on this page.

As Smilee Kylee develops programs, partnerships, and services, this page will be updated.
