Questions Surrounding Kylee's Case

Public Questions

There are parts of Kylee's story that remain unanswered, and there are other questions our family can answer from what we personally experienced.

Questions We Have Too

Questions We Cannot Answer

These are questions we believe deserve answers. We will not fill the gaps with assumptions or present unanswered questions as facts.

1
Why was Kylee living at Bethesda House in the first place?
2
Why was a pregnant 18-year-old discharged from Bethesda House after missing a meeting following a hospital visit?
3
Why was Kylee taken back to Gregory Groom's home after being discharged?
4
Who specifically made the decision to transport her there, and what information did that person have about the situation?
5
Did Bethesda House know about any previous allegations of domestic violence or safety concerns involving Gregory?
6
Why did Kylee refuse her sister's offer to come pick her up after describing an alleged assault?
7
What happened during the roughly 30 minutes between Kylee's frightening first message and her later message saying she was safe?
8
What exactly happened between Kylee's last confirmed contact with her family and the time she was reported missing?
9
Why was Gregory the person who reported Kylee missing?
10
Why did Gregory participate in efforts to find Kylee if prosecutors allege he already knew where she was?
11
Who made the apparent kidnapping/hostage call to Kylee's sisters while everyone was searching for her?
12
Why wasn't Kylee found during the earlier search of the Rehoboth property?
13
If prosecutors allege Gregory admitted killing Kylee and then provided a hand-drawn map leading investigators to where she was buried, why should this case be resolved through a plea agreement instead of a trial?
14
If the prosecution can prove that Kylee was killed, buried on the property, and that Gregory knew where her body was located, what level of accountability would truly reflect what happened to Kylee and her unborn baby?
15
What evidence will prosecutors present to a jury to prove their allegations against Gregory—and will Kylee's family have the opportunity to see the entire case heard in court?
These questions are not accusations or findings of guilt. Where a question refers to allegations made by prosecutors, those allegations remain subject to the criminal court process.
Our Family's Perspective

Questions We Can Answer

These are questions we can begin answering from our family's experiences, conversations, recollections, and the information available to us. Answers will be added to this page.

1
What was the last independently verified phone call, text, social-media activity, or other digital activity from Kylee herself?
2
Was Kylee's phone ever recovered, and if so, what did investigators learn from it?
3
What did phone-location data show about Kylee's movements during her final hours?
4
Were Gregory's phone records and location data consistent with the account he initially gave investigators?
5
Were there security cameras, doorbell cameras, traffic cameras, or other surveillance near the property that captured Kylee or Gregory?
6
Did any neighbors hear or see anything unusual around the time investigators believe Kylee died?
7
What did Kylee tell friends—not just family—about what was happening in the days before she disappeared?
8
Had anyone outside the family recently seen injuries on Kylee or heard her describe alleged violence?
9
Did Kylee tell anyone she was afraid of Gregory before the August 6 messages?
10
What belongings did Kylee have with her when she returned to the property?
11
What happened to Kylee's personal belongings after she disappeared—including Bob and her Elmo blanket?
12
Was Kylee allegedly killed where she was eventually found, or do prosecutors believe her body was moved afterward?
13
What did the medical examiner determine about Kylee's cause and manner of death, and how much of that information has been made public?
14
Was a suspected weapon recovered, and has forensic testing connected any weapon to Kylee's death?
15
What exactly is the witness-intimidation charge connected to, and who was allegedly being intimidated?
16
When did the family first realize something was seriously wrong and that Kylee might actually be missing?
17
What was Gregory telling Kylee's family directly while everyone was searching for her?
18
Did anything Gregory said or did during the search make the family suspicious at the time, or did those things only seem significant afterward?
19
What efforts did Kylee's family personally make to find her before they learned she had died?
20
Looking back now, were there things Kylee said or did in the days or weeks before her disappearance that the family understands differently today?
Important legal note:

Gregory Groom has been charged in connection with Kylee's death and has pleaded not guilty. Statements about what he allegedly told investigators or what prosecutors believe occurred are allegations and prosecution claims unless and until established in court. A criminal charge is not a conviction, and a defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.

Questions on this page are presented to identify information the family and public may want clarified. The existence of a question should not be interpreted as proof that its premise is true.
